WINTER FIELD NOTES
The Amalfi coast has a winter personality

Choose one or two towns, travel overland and leave early. Treat ferries as an unconfirmed bonus until the operator publishes your dates. Swap to Naples museums if rain or road conditions make the coast unpleasant.

Seasonal transport guidance ↗
New Year’s is a reservation, not a vibe

Book Rome rooms first, then dinner and a paid indoor party. Budget INR 15,000–20,000 per person for the full evening, inside the editable nightlife allowance. Preferred shortlist: Room26 in EUR, with admission before midnight. No 2026/27 Room26 package is confirmed here. January 1 is an easy walking day; the Vatican Museums list it as closed.

Official museum calendar ↗
Venice is a long, lovely day

Budget roughly 2.5–3 hours each way from Milan, subject to the final timetable. Choose an early outbound train and a reserved return. If that feels too rushed, replace the day with a Milan food crawl. Check the official Venice access-fee rules for your date when published.

Venice access information ↗
Swiss train tickets: compare before buying a pass

Compare point-to-point tickets with any pass against this exact route. Milan → Lucerne → Engelberg needs a timetable check and may involve additional changes. Allow a half day for the transfer. Leave Engelberg early on January 8 and aim to reach Zurich airport at least three hours before departure.

Plan with SBB ↗
Why not add the Dolomites?

With only three Swiss nights and a Zurich flight, an additional mountain base takes time away from skiing. Engelberg keeps two ski days together. If skiing becomes the whole point of the trip, trade the Milan/Venice segment for a longer mountain stay.
